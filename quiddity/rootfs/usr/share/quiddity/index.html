    Connecting a device

    1. In Quiddity on your other device, open Devices, and add the ID above.
    2. Copy that device's own ID from the same page, and add it to this app's Allowed devices on its Configuration tab. Then restart the app.
    3. They connect by themselves from then on. Once one device is let in, it can let in the rest, so you only need to do this once.
